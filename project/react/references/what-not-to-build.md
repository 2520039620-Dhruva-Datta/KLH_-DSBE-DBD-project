Patterns from large national government portals (india.gov.in and similar) that
are the WRONG fit for AMAP's PUBLIC HOMEPAGE specifically. Several of these are
fine, even encouraged, on the signed-in interior screens (§2.3a-2.3c) where the
larger dataset and returning users justify the density — this list is scoped to
the first thing a stranger sees, not to the whole product. Do not include these
on the homepage by default:

- A mega-search bar spanning dozens of unrelated categories (Acts, Datasets,
  Directory, Helplines, News...) — AMAP has one real catalogue of services;
  design for that scale, not for a national index.
- A wall of quantified stat cards (service counts, scheme counts, engagement
  counts) used as a trust signal through sheer number. A small, honest, real
  number (e.g. real applications processed in the seed data) beats a wall of
  impressive-looking ones.
- Rotating hero carousels, autoplay testimonial sliders, or "spotlight of the
  month" narrative modules — these exist to fill space on a portal with far
  more content than navigation can otherwise surface. AMAP doesn't have that
  problem, so it doesn't need that solution.
- Thematic "explore" content unrelated to the core task (tourism, culture,
  regional-product spotlights) — charming on a national portal, off-topic here.
- A permanent multi-tool accessibility toolbar with many switches — keep the
  handful of controls that matter, presented simply, not a control panel.
- Eighteen-plus category navigation — AMAP's departments are few; show them as
  what they are, don't inflate the taxonomy to look more comprehensive.

The underlying mistake all of these share, ON A HOMEPAGE: designing for the
appearance of scale and comprehensiveness instead of for how quickly a real
person understands and completes their task. Optimize for the latter — on the
homepage. Once inside the product, real scale (a large dataset, real search,
real filtering, real history) is not just acceptable but the explicit goal of
§2.3a-2.3c; the mistake would be under-building there out of an overcorrection
from this list.
